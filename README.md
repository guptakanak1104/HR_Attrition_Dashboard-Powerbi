# HR Attrition Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard created in Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, turnover patterns, job-related factors, and employee wellness to understand workforce trends and attrition.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Demographics
Analyzes employee demographics and workforce distribution through:
- Total Employees, Attrition Count, and Employees Who Stayed
- Male and Female Employee Attrition
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Distance From Home
- Attrition by Work-Life Balance
- Attrition by Marital Status

### 2. Turnover Analysis I
Provides insights into employee turnover across different organizational factors:
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Years in Current Role
- Job Role-wise Attrition Count

### 3. Turnover Analysis II
Explores additional factors related to employee attrition:
- Monthly Income by Job Role
- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Performance Rating
- Attrition by Job Level
- Attrition by Age and Gender

### 4. Employee Wellness
Examines employee satisfaction and workplace-related factors:
- Attrition by Environment Satisfaction
- Attrition by Relationship Satisfaction
- Attrition by Job Satisfaction
- Attrition by Job Involvement
- Attrition by Performance Rating
- Attrition by Work-Life Balance

## Key Insights

The dashboard presents an overview of 1,470 employees, including:

- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Employees Who Stayed:** 1,233

It allows users to explore attrition patterns across different demographics, job roles, departments, and employee satisfaction levels.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand employee attrition patterns, identify workforce trends, and explore the factors associated with employee turnover. It aims to present HR data through interactive visualizations that support better workforce analysis and data-driven decision-making.
